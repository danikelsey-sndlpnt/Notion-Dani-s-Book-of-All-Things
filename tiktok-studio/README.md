# Sookie's TikTok Studio

A browser video editor for TikToks, plus shoppable links from the Shopify store. Published version: https://claude.ai/artifact/6LmWcSwbqy4ecVJJdQ1nK2

## What it does

- **Clips**: add one or more videos, reorder, trim, and change speed. Add a music file and set the volume of the clips' own sound and the music separately.
- **Formats**: Talking hook, Slow stitch ASMR, New arrivals haul, Stitch timelapse and POV text box. Each one sets cuts, zooms, flashes, color look, grain, progress bar and text style, and you can change any of them.
- **Auto edits**: cuts out pauses in clips where you talk, punch-in zoom on cuts, pulses on the beat of the music, and a cap on how long a shot can run.
- **Words**: hook text, captions that pop word by word (timed automatically), and an end card. "Write hook and captions" asks Claude to write them.
- **Shop links**: search your Shopify products, put a price tag on the video, and get links tagged `utm_source=tiktok` so Shopify reports show which sales came from TikTok. Builds the post caption too.
- **Export**: records a 1080×1920 video (MP4 where the browser supports it, otherwise WebM) and saves it.

Everything runs in your browser. Your videos are not uploaded anywhere.
