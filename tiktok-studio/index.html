How to make the post shoppable on TikTok
  1. TikTok Shop: if your Shopify store is linked to TikTok Shop through the TikTok app in Shopify, tap Add link → Product when posting and pick the same products listed above.
  2. No TikTok Shop yet: put the tracked link in your bio (or your link-in-bio page) and keep the end card saying "link in bio".
  3. The tracked links tag visits with utm_source=tiktok. In Shopify, open Analytics → Reports → Sessions by referrer or Sales by UTM to see what each video sold.
  4. Products showing 0 or less in stock will disappoint buyers. Restock or pick something else before posting.
