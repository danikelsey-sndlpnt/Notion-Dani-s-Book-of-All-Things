# Canvas Count

A stock counter for the shop that checks your counts against Square. Published version: https://claude.ai/artifact/62H1Asa1EMhbj9d4biuGve

## What it does

- **Count**: canvases grouped by where they hang (Christmas wall, Pink wall, Charm display, New arrivals table, Back stock). Tap + and − to count. Each one shows Square's number next to yours.
- **Seen in photo**: canvases read from your photos come in with the count that showed in the picture. Check each one on the wall, then press Confirm.
- **Fix in Square**:
  - *Square is off*: your confirmed counts that don't match Square. Press Review update to see Square's latest numbers. Nothing changes until you press Update Square, which records your counts as a physical stock count.
  - *On the wall, not in Square*: canvases that need a listing before you can ring them up.
  - *Below zero in Square*: items Square shows as sold past zero.
  - *Look like duplicates*: two listings for one canvas, which splits its stock.
- **Add from photo**: upload up to 5 photos at a time. Claude reads the labels and designer codes and matches each canvas to Square. You review the list before anything is saved.
- **Save as spreadsheet**: exports your count as a .csv file.

Square reads and updates happen on claude.ai with your Square connection. Thread colors (items with many variations) aren't counted here yet.
