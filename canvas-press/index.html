Artwork

Read a painted canvas takes a photo, scan or listing picture of a canvas that's already painted. It finds every intersection and turns its color into a stitch you can edit and print on a new canvas.

PNG, JPG, PSD or PDF. Each file becomes a layer, and the layers are flattened into one picture. Open stitch file takes an image with one pixel per stitch, or an OXS chart, with no color changes.

Carrier sheet

Print once on plain letter paper at 100% / Actual Size. Tape your canvas inside the black window and leave the four corner dots uncovered. The dots tell the app exactly where the page sits in the scan, so each scan stands on its own. Reuse the sheet as long as the dots stay clean.
