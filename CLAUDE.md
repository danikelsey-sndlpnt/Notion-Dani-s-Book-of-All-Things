# Working with Dani

- Dani does **not** use Notion. Never suggest saving, creating or syncing anything to Notion, even though the repo name mentions it and a Notion connector may be attached.
- Dani keeps everything in **Daybook**. When something should be saved or kept, offer Daybook (or a file in this repo).
- Business: Sookie's Needlepoint.

## Brand inspo (from Pinterest board "Brand website", pinterest.com/danikelsey07/brand-website)

Retro, playful, garden-y and handmade. Pink leads, olive/sage grounds it, warm apricot/honey accents, small pops of teal or periwinkle. Vintage serif or script headlines with a clean sans body. Busy patterns (stripes, folk florals, needlepoint-style diamonds) as frames and dividers, calm cream space elsewhere. Closest website reference: September Sun Candle Co.

Starter palette:

| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Main pink (Cherry Blossom) | `#FFB7C5` |
| Deep pink, buttons/links | `#E44587` |
| Olive | `#8A8635` |
| Soft green (Duck Egg) | `#9BBCAC` |
| Apricot | `#F09367` |
| Honey | `#F6C96B` |
| Backgrounds | `#FFE3EC`, `#FCF3EA` |

Fonts: Playfair Display, Cinzel or Brasika for headlines; Pacifico, Yellowtail or Whimsy Foliage for script accents; Montserrat or Quicksand for body.
