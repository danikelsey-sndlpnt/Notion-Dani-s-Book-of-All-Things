# TikTok Supply Scanner

Turns a TikTok into a shopping list. Paste the link and the caption (and add screenshots of the supplies if you can), press **Find what I need**, and Claude lists the materials, tools and ingredients, grouped and with brands, colors and sizes when the post shows them.

## How to use it

1. Open the TikTok, expand the caption and copy it. A pinned comment with a supply list is worth copying too.
2. Optional but helpful: pause where the supplies, labels or on-screen text show and take screenshots.
3. Paste the link and text, add screenshots, then press **Find what I need**.
4. Tick what you already have. **Copy what I still need** gives you a plain checklist for notes or texts.

Lists are saved under **Saved lists**. On claude.ai they're saved to the page and follow you across devices. Opened as a plain file, they save in that browser only, and scanning needs claude.ai.

## Why it doesn't read the video by itself

The page can't reach TikTok directly: artifact pages have no outside network access, and TikTok blocks automated downloads. So it works from what you paste and screenshot. Items Claude has to guess are marked "Claude's guess".
