# Dani's Money Book

A single-page money tracker for the shop and for home. Open `index.html` in a browser, or use the published version on claude.ai (where your data is saved to the page and follows you across devices). Opened as a plain file, it saves in that browser only.

## What it does

- **Next moves**: a short list of what to handle now, such as a dip below your cash cushion, late bills, budgets running hot, the tax set-aside, and goal pacing.
- **Balances and a 90-day forecast** for Shop and Personal, built from your bills, paydays and typical monthly sales and spending. Shows the lowest point and when you'd drop under your cushion.
- **Money in & out**: add transactions, "Pay myself" (logs the owner's draw out of the shop and owner's pay into personal in one step), and import a bank CSV with a review step.
- **Bills & paydays**: recurring items (weekly to yearly). "Paid" records the payment and moves the bill to its next date.
- **Tax set-aside**: shop profit so far this year × your rate, minus estimated taxes paid, with the next federal quarterly due date.
- **Budgets and savings goals** per book.
- **Backup**: export transactions (.csv) or a full backup (.json), and restore from one.

It starts with example numbers. Go to **Setup**, enter your real balances and press **Start my books**.
